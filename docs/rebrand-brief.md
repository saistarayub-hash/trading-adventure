# 🌿 Rebrand brief — "Northern Lights Herb" edition

**Status: BRIEF ONLY — nothing built yet. The companion app is finalized at v1.1.0 first.**

This document captures what the client wants for the next phase, what is technically and legally
possible, and the exact assets we need from them before any of it goes into the app.

## What we CAN do (and will, happily)

1. **DOC as the main character.** A hand-drawn / vector cartoon character named DOC who coaches,
   narrates lessons, reacts to quizzes and hosts the journal. Character slots already exist in the
   app (Professor Fox is one), so this is a theming + content job, not a rewrite.
2. **A full brand theme pack.** Colours, typography vibe, splash screen, app icon, bubble-mode
   pill, card styling — redrawn from the brand's *public aesthetic* (we look at the public page
   like any designer would and recreate the vibe in original art; we do not copy assets).
3. **Cannabis-culture flavour content.** Original illustrations (leaf motifs, smoke swirls, grow-room
   scenes), flavour text, lesson names, quiz banter — written by us, owned by the client.
4. **Cartoon avatars of the crew** — original characters *inspired by* the crew's roles and vibes
   ("the grower", "the roller", "the hype one"), not copies of anyone's face.
5. **An 18+ age gate** and a compliance footer on every screen.

## What we will NOT do (and why — this protects the client)

1. **Scrape Instagram** for photos, videos or faces. It violates Instagram's terms, the downloads
   are watermarked/compressed, and — most importantly —
2. **Use real people's likenesses without written permission.** A person's face is theirs. Putting
   the crew (or anyone tagged in their videos) into an app needs a signed **model release** from
   each person, and any footage needs a **content licence** from whoever shot it. Without that the
   client is exposed to takedowns and claims — exactly what a growing brand cannot afford.
   *The safe routes:* (a) original cartoon avatars (no paperwork), or (b) the client hands us
   photos/videos they already own **plus** a one-page release signed by each person shown.
3. **Medical or health claims** about cannabis anywhere in the app ("cures", "treats", dosing
   advice). Education/entertainment/brand only.
4. **Anything aimed at minors.** The existing kid-friendly Professor Fox app and this brand edition
   must stay separate products; the brand edition gets an age gate and its own packaging identity.

## Legal context (plain language, not legal advice)

- **South Africa:** the Cannabis for Private Purposes Act (2024) legalises *private* use and
  cultivation; **buying and selling cannabis without a licence is still illegal**. So the app must
  never facilitate sales, orders, prices or deliveries — brand, education and entertainment only.
  A one-line "we do not sell; private-use education only" compliance note is cheap insurance.
- **App stores:** Google Play restricts cannabis-related apps heavily; a sideloaded APK or a PWA
  (both already built) sidesteps store policy entirely. If a store listing is ever wanted, that is
  a separate compliance project.
- **Music in the brand's videos** is almost never owned by the brand — never reuse video audio.
